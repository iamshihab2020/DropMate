# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-25)

### Photos and videos
- A gallery button next to the message box. On Android it opens the phone's photo picker, so you can pick many photos and videos at once; on Windows it opens a file dialog showing only photos and videos.
- Finished photos and videos show as pictures in the chat instead of a file icon. Several from one send show as a grid, with "+N" for the rest, and videos get a play badge.
- Tap a picture to open it full screen. Swipe, or use the arrow keys on the PC, to go through every photo and video in the chat. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Videos open in your video player.

### Buzz
- A Buzz button in the chat header, while the person is online. Their phone rings at full alarm volume, even on silent, and vibrates for up to 30 seconds, with a full-screen card and a Stop button.
- In My devices, the same button rings your own devices, to find your phone.

### Delivery
- Ticks on your messages and files: Waiting, Received, and Seen once the person opens the chat.
- Finished transfers show how long they took and how fast they went, for example "1.2 GB in 38 s · 32 MB/s".

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-24)

First release.

- Pair once with a QR code or a short code, checked by four matching emojis.
- One conversation per person across all their devices, with messages, files and folders of any size.
- Approve each transfer, or agree with a person to skip approval up to a size you choose. Apps and scripts always ask.
- Link your own laptop and phones: chats, contacts and settings sync, and files move between them without asking.
- Transfers resume where they stopped and check every chunk. Several chunks travel at once, hashed on a pool of background isolates.
- Receives with the app closed or the phone locked on Android, and from the tray on Windows.
- Drag and drop, Ctrl+V and an Explorer "Send with DropMate" menu on Windows; the share sheet on Android.
- Profile photos, search, nicknames, favourites, blocking, a speed limit and history export.
- English and Bangla, light and dark themes, and a redesigned interface with the liquid-fill transfer animation.
