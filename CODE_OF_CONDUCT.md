# Code of Conduct

DropMate is a friendly place to build something useful. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives, is expected to follow this code.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that the maintainer makes the final call on what goes into the project.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing an idea after it has been declined.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, contact the maintainer, [@iamshihab2020](https://github.com/iamshihab2020), privately. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or block anyone who breaks this code.
